Source code for the OpenSurge engine in this archive
====================================================

The program Engine/opensurge is Open Surge Engine 0.6.1.3 (Alexandre
Martins and contributors), ported to AmigaOS 3 by the AmiCradle Team. It is
free software under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later
(Licences/GPL-3.0.txt). It was built from:

  @ENGINE_REPO@ at commit
  @ENGINE_COMMIT@
  (tag @ENGINE_TAG@)
  https://github.com/@ENGINE_REPO@/tree/@ENGINE_COMMIT@

with its own scripts (ports/amigaos3/build-dependencies.sh,
build-first-pass.sh, package.sh), which fetch and pin every library linked
into it:

  SurgeScript 0.6.1      https://github.com/alemart/surgescript (v0.6.1)
  PhysicsFS 3.2.0        https://github.com/icculus/physfs (release-3.2.0)
  libogg 1.3.5           https://github.com/xiph/ogg (v1.3.5)
  libvorbis 1.3.7        https://github.com/xiph/vorbis (v1.3.7)
  Allegro 5 (Amiga port) https://github.com/DalsinAI/amigaallegro
                         commit @ALLEGRO_COMMIT@

The complete corresponding source is available from those addresses at no
charge for as long as this archive is offered. If any of them stops
working, ask the AmiCradle Team through
https://github.com/@ENGINE_REPO@/issues for a copy.

The engine's game assets in Engine/ (graphics, sounds, music, fonts) are
under the licences in Engine/licenses/, as listed in Engine/README.md and
upstream's CONTRIBUTING.md.

The launcher, Installer script and packaging (StartGame, Install-*,
Tools/) are MIT-licensed (Licences/Port-scripts-MIT.txt); their source is
the port's repository at commit @PORT_COMMIT@.
